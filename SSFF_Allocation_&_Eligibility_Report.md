# Egypt Sovereign Sustainable Financing Framework (SSFF) — Allocation & Eligibility Audit

**Cut-off:** 3 November 2026, end of day, Africa/Cairo.  
**Research conducted:** 4 November 2026. Amounts below retain their source units and reporting dates.

The seven Panda allocations reconcile to **EGP 14,782 million**. However, the evidence does **not** support calling the entire dataset “100% verified.” Material qualifications are:

- **Chapter 6 is a pipeline scope restriction, not an SSFF-wide eligibility requirement.** The framework also permits certain subsidies, tax expenditures and operating expenditures.
- **The timing rules use budget years.** They must not automatically become rolling 36-month and 24-month tests.
- **ISS-Corporate explicitly disputes the Cairo–Assiut road’s clean-transport eligibility.**
- Published project-level **CNY allocations**, project-level **refinancing splits**, Moody’s numerical **governance/sustainability weights**, and an instrument-specific **onshore AAA rating** remain unverified.

### B3. Quantitative and governance rules

| Rule | Verified finding | Dataset/engine consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Refinancing lookback | Expenditure may fall in the **three budget years preceding issuance** | Compare fiscal-year identifiers; do not subtract 1,095 days |
| Issuance-year expenditure | Included | Retain expenditure date and issuance fiscal year |
| Forward expenditure window | Issuance budget year plus **two following budget years** | Distinguish eligible expenditure window from allocation completion date |
| “24-month commitment” | SPO describes allocation within no more than two budget years following issuance; **a universal rolling 24-calendar-month deadline was not established** | Do not hard-code `IssueDate + 24 months` as an SSFF covenant |
| Eligible expenditure types | Investments, subsidies, tax expenditures and selected operating expenditure | Chapter 1–5 cannot be rejected solely because they are outside Chapter 6 under the full SSFF |
| Chapter 6 pipeline | Valid scope choice for this project | Label rejection `OUTSIDE CHAPTER 6 PIPELINE`, not `SSFF PROHIBITS OPERATING SPEND` |
| Separate ESG capital-market issuers | State disbursements to local agencies/authorities raising their own ESG-related financing are excluded | Require recipient-level financing check |
| SFWG | MoF chairs; selection/allocation reviews are semiannual; continuing eligibility monitored | Require documented approval and review dates |
| Environmental/social controls | ESIA and relevant EEAA permission required | Missing evidence produces `PENDING VERIFICATION` |
| Proceeds | General funding accounts, with earmarking and Sustainable Financing Register | Bank-account commingling does not eliminate allocation tracking |
| Reporting | Annual allocation/impact reporting until full allocation; material-change reporting; external review commitment | Maintain reporting and assurance scope separately |

Sources: SSFF pp.16, 30–35, 39; Moody’s SPO pp.3–5. [assets.mof.gov.eg](https://assets.mof.gov.eg/files/2024-01/04e0af50-bdb8-11ee-b3f8-9dc3ae2f33b1.pdf)

**IFI/MDB adjustment.** No explicit universal “deduct all IFI loans” formula was located in the SSFF. For the proposed screening engine, use a separately labelled **anti-double-allocation control**:

\[
\text{Available eligible expenditure}
=
\max(0,\ \text{documented eligible expenditure}
-\text{other financing of the same expenditure}
-\text{prior sustainable allocations})
\]

Deduct only overlapping amounts on the **same expenditure perimeter**. MDB involvement elsewhere in a project does not make the whole project ineligible. A guarantee is credit enhancement, not a second project disbursement.

**Moody’s findings.** `SQS2 — Very Good` is verified. The opinion identifies limited proceeds-distribution visibility, insufficient technical specificity in some subcategories, and labour/human-rights challenges. It weights the 14 categories evenly for its expected-contribution assessment. **Numerical weights between governance/alignment and sustainability contribution were not verified and remain blank.** [media.mof.gov.eg](https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/Second_Party_Opinion_Moodys_MIS_9_Nov_2022_a473d773ac.pdf)

**Panda credit enhancement.** AIIB’s stated limit is USD 200 million equivalent and AfDB’s USD 345 million equivalent, including covered principal/interest. The PPOM describes several—not joint—guarantees and an aggregate maximum of **RMB 3,911,955,500**. These figures are guarantee limits, not additional proceeds. An actual instrument-level Chinese onshore AAA rating and rating-agency report were **not verified**; the guarantors’ AAA status cannot substitute for that evidence.
